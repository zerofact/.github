## How an engagement runs

| Stage | What happens | You get | Typical time |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **01 · Discovery** | We sit with the people doing the work and map the process as it actually runs. | Process map, risk list, scope options | 1–2 weeks |
| **02 · Blueprint** | Architecture, data model, integration plan, fixed scope and a written estimate. | Technical blueprint + quote | 1–2 weeks |
| **03 · Build** | Two-week increments. Working software you can open and use at the end of each one. | Staging environment, demo every sprint | 6–16 weeks |
| **04 · Rollout** | Data migration, parallel run, staff training, cutover with a rollback plan. | Live system, trained users, handbook | 2–4 weeks |
| **05 · Run** | Monitoring, backups, patches, enhancements on a monthly retainer. | SLA-backed support | Ongoing |

You can stop after any stage. The blueprint is yours whether or not we build it.

## What we commit to

- **You own everything.** Source code, infrastructure accounts, domains and data are in your name from day one.
- **No lock-in.** Standard tooling, documented deployment, and a handover pack any competent team can pick up.
- **Security is not a phase.** Least-privilege access, encrypted data at rest and in transit, audit trails on financial and record changes, dependency scanning in CI.
- **Tested where it counts.** Automated coverage on money, permissions and anything that touches a legal record.
- **Written down.** Architecture notes, runbooks and admin guides ship with the software, not after it.
- **One point of contact.** A named lead engineer for the life of the engagement.
